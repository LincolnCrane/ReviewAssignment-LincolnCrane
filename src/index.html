Accessibility Commitment

Designing with accessibility in mind is an essential part of my development process.

Inclusive Design Principles

I strive to create websites that are usable and accessible to as many people as possible, including users with disabilities. This includes thoughtful layout decisions, readable typography, and clear visual hierarchy to support ease of use.

Accessibility is not an afterthought in my work, but an ongoing consideration throughout the design and development process.

Accessibility Practices

  • Use of semantic HTML elements to support screen readers
  • Meaningful alt text for images and media
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and interactive elements
  • Sufficient color contrast for readability
  • ARIA attributes used when appropriate to enhance accessibility
